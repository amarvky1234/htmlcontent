Rafael Jet Description

The Dassault Rafale is a 4.5-generation, twin-engine, canard-delta wing omnirole fighter designed by France for air superiority, deep strikes, and nuclear deterrence. Known for high agility, low radar cross-section, and the SPECTRA electronic warfare suite, it can carry a wide range of weapons including Meteor, SCALP, and Exocet missiles
